### John von Seggern

<!-- copy:founders.john.name -->
John von Seggern

<!-- copy:founders.john.role -->
Founder, Futureproof Music School

<!-- copy:founders.john.bio_1 -->
John founded Futureproof Music School, where he built Kadence, an AI music-production tutor, and Nova, an operations agent that lets a core team of four operate like a team of twenty. He has run online education at three of the best-known music production schools: as Director of Online Education he tripled Icon Collective’s online student population over seven-plus years, grew Dubspot’s online enrollments by more than 50% (including a complex migration to a new course platform), and built Point Blank’s online community to 5,000+ members. He was putting AI to work in education years before founding Futureproof: at Icon he built an early chatbot for music production questions and used AI for course creation and student sentiment analysis.

<!-- copy:founders.john.bio_2 -->

He wrote the books Ableton Live 6 Power! and Laptop Music Power!, plus the official reference manual for the Massive synthesizer, and has sound design credits on major films including WALL-E. He holds an MA in Ethnomusicology from UC Riverside and a BA in Music from Carleton College.
