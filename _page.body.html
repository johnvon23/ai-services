SWJ–01 / Opportunities

Where the work usually gets stuck.

Every music company is different — a label, a publisher, a distributor, a venue, an artist business, a school — but the highest-value opportunities usually appear in the same three places.

Handoffs and follow-through

Stop work from falling through.

Capture inquiries, releases, partner requests, and next steps, then follow up without somebody manually chasing them.

Recurring work

Give your team the routine work back.

Automate reports, checks, data entry, reminders, and handoffs so the team starts the day with finished work.

Connected decisions

See what actually drives the business.

Connect marketing, catalog, sales, and operations so you can see what is working.

Human control

Anything involving judgment, money, or artist and partner communication stays under human control.

SWJ–04 / Founders

You will work directly with us.

Both of us come from music production and music technology — not from outside consulting. Between us: two decades at Dubspot, Point Blank, Icon Collective, 343 Labs, and Native Instruments, a music school founded, a music technology company founded, and two AI products built from scratch.

John von Seggern

Founder, Futureproof Music School

John founded Futureproof Music School, where he built Kadence, an AI music-production tutor, and Nova, an operations agent that lets a core team of four operate like a team of twenty. He has run online education at three of the best-known music production schools: as Director of Online Education he tripled Icon Collective’s online student population over seven-plus years, grew Dubspot’s online enrollments by more than 50% — including a complex migration to a new course platform — and built Point Blank’s online community to 5,000+ members. He was putting AI to work in education years before founding Futureproof, building an early AI chatbot for music production at Icon alongside AI-assisted course creation and student sentiment analysis.

Earlier, John co-founded Modus Institute, an online school teaching Lean process improvement, and spent six years managing Native Instruments’ North American support team. He wrote two books on music production — Ableton Live 6 Power! and Laptop Music Power! — plus the official reference manual for the Massive synthesizer, and contributed sound design to Pixar’s WALL-E, Wreck-It Ralph, Moana, and Cars 3. He holds an MA in Ethnomusicology from UC Riverside and a BA in Music from Carleton College.
